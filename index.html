OlympiadEdge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026 (v2)

In short: OlympiadEdge does not collect any personal information. There are no ads, no tracking and no sign-up. The student's first name, class and test scores are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

1. Who we are

OlympiadEdge is an Olympiad and school practice app made by Shabanam Bano Ansari (Shabanam Tutoring), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. The app does not ask for an email address, phone number, school, photo, location, or contacts. There is no account or login.

3. Information saved on your device only

To make the app work, some information is saved only on your own device:

  • The student's first name or nickname and class, used only to greet the student and show quizzes for the right class. A nickname is fine.
  • Test results (scores, time taken, medals) so you can see progress and your best score.
  • The date the subscription was last confirmed, so Premium can keep working for a short time without internet.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is not copied to cloud backup. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Internet, ads and analytics

The app does not show advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking tools. The app itself does not request internet access; question banks are included inside the app.

5. Payments

Premium is an optional monthly subscription sold through Google Play. All payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store card, UPI, or bank details. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can cancel the subscription anytime in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

6. Children

OlympiadEdge is designed for students of Classes 1 to 10. Because we collect no personal information, no child's personal data is processed by us. We ask parents or guardians to approve any subscription purchase.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, adding online features), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.
